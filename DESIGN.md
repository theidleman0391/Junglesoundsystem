## Brand & Style

This design system captures an immersive, high-end nightlife and sonic festival experience. Rooted in the convergence of mystical cosmic darkness, bioluminescent flora, and sharp geometric luxury, it creates an atmosphere that feels simultaneously primal and futuristic.

The brand targets discerning electronic music enthusiasts, international festival goers, audio purists, and VIP patrons. The emotional response evokes awe, nocturnal energy, exclusivity, and raw acoustic power wrapped in metallic finesse.

The visual style blends **Atmospheric Dark Glassmorphism** with **Geometric Luxury**. Deep nebula voids serve as the backdrop, layered with smoky frosted-glass panels, razor-sharp metallic gold lines inspired by the faceted jaguar emblem, and neon chromatic edge highlights echoing tropical flora under blacklight.

## Colors

The palette is tuned specifically for low-light digital environments, utilizing pitch obsidian and deep cosmic indigo as the grounding field. 

- **Primary (`#D4AF37`)**: Warm brushed bullion gold derived from the geometric jaguar crest. Used for focal CTAs, badges of status, active states, key branding elements, and sharp metallic highlights.
- **Secondary (`#00E59B`)**: Electric emerald neon. Evokes bioluminescent canopy leaves, active audio visualizers, positive metrics, and vibrant nocturnal energy.
- **Tertiary (`#B026FF`)**: Cosmic orchid ultraviolet. Channels deep twilight canopy illumination, live stage lasers, secondary accents, and interactive glows.
- **Neutral (`#08070F`)**: Deep cosmic obsidian. An ultra-dark navy-violet foundation that prevents true-black sterility, providing depth for translucent glass layering.

Surfaces use stepped opacities over neutral backgrounds (`rgba(18, 15, 29, 0.65)` for mid-tier cards, `rgba(29, 23, 48, 0.85)` for elevated controls) layered with ambient specular gradients of gold and violet.

## Typography

The type system blends the technical, faceted modernism of **Space Grotesk** with the fluid, legible warmth of **Outfit**.

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Geometric, sharp, and confident. Evokes architectural line work, audio synthesizer interfaces, and the angular facets of the signature jaguar icon.
- **Body & Paragraphs (`Outfit`)**: Balanced geometric grotesque with humanistic open apertures. Ensures effortless legibility against dark, luminous, and frosted glass backgrounds.
- **Labels, Metadata & Track Info (`Space Grotesk`)**: Set with wide tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) and uppercase treatment to convey a luxury stage-production and audio-hardware spec aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system uses a flexible 12-column grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a 4-column responsive grid on mobile devices. 

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with a 24px (`1.5rem`) gutter and generous 48px (`3rem`) margins to establish an open, editorial stage layout.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with 20px gutters and 32px margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins.

Spacing follows an intentional vertical cadence: audio players and stage lineup cards maintain generous breathing room to prioritize visual punch over extreme compaction. Generous section gaps let dark cosmic backdrop visuals filter through without visual overcrowding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use standard drop shadows; instead, it utilizes **translucent glass tiers, luminous border filaments, and spectral color bleeds**.

1. **Surface 0 (Base Canvas)**: Solid `#08070F` with ambient, fixed radial background gradients of ultraviolet (`#B026FF`) and botanical cyan/green (`#00E59B`) at 8%–14% opacity.
2. **Surface 1 (Cards & Modules)**: Frosted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), filled with `rgba(18, 15, 29, 0.6)`. Outlined by a 1px border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Surface 2 (Elevated & Hover States)**: `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, filled with `rgba(26, 21, 41, 0.8)`. Bounded by dual-light borders (`top: rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45)`, `bottom: rgba(176, 38, 255, 0.2)`). Box shadow uses soft colored ambient light: `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5), 0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.
4. **Surface 3 (Overlays, Drawers & Modals)**: Frosted deep tint (`backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, background `rgba(11, 9, 20, 0.92)`), framed with a distinct 1px gold border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.6)`) and an outer neon bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 40px rgba(176, 38, 255, 0.2)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs **Soft Shape Language (`roundedness: 1`)**. 

To honor the geometric facets of the jaguar emblem and technical audio equipment, corner radiuses are kept refined and controlled rather than overly circular:
- Micro elements (tags, badges, checkboxes): `4px` (`0.25rem`).
- Standard elements (buttons, inputs, cards): `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- Large dialogs and stage modules: `12px` (`0.75rem`).
- Circular exceptions are strictly preserved for audio transport controls (Play/Pause triggers), avatar roundels, and frequency knob rotary sliders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Apex Gold)**: Background rendered in metallic gold `#D4AF37` with dark `#08070F` bold typography (`Space Grotesk`). Hover triggers an internal luminosity shift and a gold outer aura (`0 0 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary (Neon Ghost)**: Translucent dark fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), 1px border in `#00E59B`, and text colored in vibrant neon emerald. Hover brings a soft inner emerald wash (`rgba(0, 229, 155, 0.12)`).
- **Tertiary (Ghost Violet)**: Transparent body with 1px border of `rgba(176, 38, 255, 0.3)` and `#E0B0FF` text.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed with frosted glass panels (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- Features a subtle 1px border gradient transitioning from metallic gold at the top-left corner to deep purple or neon green at the bottom-right.
- Hover interactions lift the component via `transform: translateY(-2px)` accompanied by an intensified perimeter glow.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact indicators featuring uppercase `label-sm` tracking.
- Styled with dark translucent fills, thin metallic borders (`0.5px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`), and an optional glowing status dot (emerald for "Live/On Air", gold for "VIP Tier", violet for "Cosmic Stage").

### Input Fields
- Dark slate-tinted translucent fill (`rgba(13, 11, 22, 0.75)`) with 1px low-contrast border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- Focused state transitions the border to illuminated primary gold (`#D4AF37`) with a faint 3px outer diffusion blur (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Geometric square or diamond-accented contours for checkboxes, circular rings for radio buttons.
- Inactive: Bordered in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`.
- Active: Filled with `#D4AF37` on checks or `#00E59B` on radios, with a sharp `#08070F` check glyph and ambient focal bloom.

### Lists & Audio Queues
- Row items separated by delicate ambient hairline rules (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Active or currently playing tracks reveal an emerald waveform equalizer indicator, a subtle violet row background gradient, and gold typographic highlight.
